    About the Measurement

    This page measures the performance of a terrestrial (wired, ground-based) network connection at a single site on the KAIST campus in Daejeon, South Korea. It serves as a baseline for comparison with the Starlink measurements. It will publish latency, throughput (download and upload speed), and packet loss, and show how they change over time.

    • Project: KAIST–ACAI Starlink Monitor
    • Measurement Location: KAIST, Daejeon, South Korea
